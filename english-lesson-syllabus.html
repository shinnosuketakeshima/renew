英語レッスンシラバス

スリランカにおける1週間の例を、サンプルとしてご紹介いたします。担当の先生・参加日程・英語レベル・レッスン合計時間により実際の進め方やスケジュールは異なります。

スリランカ（曜日別の例）

Monday（月曜）

Free time

15:30～18:45 個人レッスン

  1. Discussion
    • Self-Introduction
    • Vocabulary expansion, Pronunciations, Verb tenses
    • Questions: Yes/No questions, Why questions, Information Questions, Tag questions
  2. Listening activity
    • Vocabulary expansion, Communicative listening, Pronunciations
  3. Discussion
    • Talking about people (physical appearance, qualities), things and places
    • Talking about similarities, differences and assessing person’s good and bad qualities

Tuesday（火曜）

9:00～12:15 個人レッスン

  • Talking about family and relations
  • Frequency expressions (e.g. Always, Often, Sometimes)
  • Pronunciations
  • Pair work or Group work practice (e.g. Role-plays, Discussions, and Presentations)
  • Grammar patterns
  • Simple writing task

15:30～18:45 個人レッスン

  1. Reviewing
  2. Discussion — Asking about preference indications. Expressing feelings, emotions (fear, anger, disgust) and ideas. Vocabulary expansion.
  3. Language game
  4. Picture description — Verb tenses, Vocabulary expansion

Wednesday（水曜）

Free time

15:30～18:45 個人レッスン

  1. Reviewing
  2. Listening activity (a song, a story, a speech)
  3. Discussion — Talking about experiences (still, yet, never, ever)
  4. Discussion on newspaper topics

Thursday（木曜）

9:00～12:15 個人レッスン

  • Talking about past events, past time experiences (e.g. Simple past tense)
  • Talking about things that have just happened (e.g. Present perfect tense)
  • Speculating about the past
  • Language game or activity

15:30～18:45 個人レッスン

  1. Reviewing
  2. Giving directions — Discussion on process description (can, should, will, might, may, must)
  3. Activity Story Telling — Reporting what someone said, what someone asked
  4. Discussion — Comparing (e.g. things between Japan and Sri Lanka)

Friday（金曜）

Free time

15:30～18:45 個人レッスン

  1. Reviewing
  2. Role play for real communication (e.g. Going to the doctor) — Talking about parts of the body, pains, and remedies. Asking for and giving advice, making suggestions, agreeing and disagreeing with suggestions.
  3. Discussion — Talking about cultural events. Talking about Arts, Writers, Artists and Performances.
  4. Demonstration lesson (e.g. Cooking demonstration, Shopping, Going to book store or fruit stall, etc.) — Vocabulary expansion

You are learning English. Many different activities in our program will help you to reach your goal. Good luck! Enjoy learning!

現地の言葉・シンハラ語レッスンも可

シンハラ語レッスンもご希望の場合は、ご受講いただけます。初心者の方でも丁寧に先生が英語を介してシンハラ語を教えてくださいます。英語とシンハラ語の両方を学びたい方は、ご希望に合わせてカリキュラムを設定できます（例：英語8割、シンハラ語2割等）。実際のスケジュールは現地で先生と相談して決めます。

レッスンの際は、午前・午後ともに15分程度の Tea Break がそれぞれ1回ずつあります。また、紅茶を飲みながらレッスンをさせていただくことがあります。

レッスンのない時間帯には、スリランカのボランティア活動への参加も可能です）。
